# Manage your account

## Change the email on your account
Go to Settings, then Profile, then Email. Enter the new address and confirm it from the link we send
to it. Until you confirm, sign-in keeps using the old address.

## Update your name or profile details
Go to Settings, then Profile. Changes to your name and photo save right away and show on new
activity.

## Delete your account
> Handled by a person.

Account deletion cannot be undone and removes all data. A person on our team must verify the
request, so the agent always hands deletion requests to a human.

## Add or remove team members
Workspace admins can invite people from Settings, then Team, then Invite. To remove someone, open
their row and choose Remove. Removed members lose access immediately; their content stays in the
workspace.
