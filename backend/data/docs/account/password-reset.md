# Reset your password

## Reset a forgotten password
On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password" and enter the email on your account. We send a reset
link that works for 30 minutes. Open it, choose a new password, and sign in again. Check your spam
folder if the email does not arrive within 5 minutes.

## Reset link expired or not working
Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once. Request a new link from the sign-in page.
If the new link also fails, clear your browser cache or try a private window.

## Change your password while signed in
Go to Settings, then Security, then Change password. Enter your current password and the new one.
Other devices stay signed in until their session expires.
