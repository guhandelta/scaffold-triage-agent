# Plans and invoices

## Find and download invoices
Go to Settings, then Billing, then Invoices. Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. Invoices are
also emailed to the billing contact each month.

## Change your plan
Workspace admins can upgrade or downgrade from Settings, then Billing, then Plan. Upgrades take
effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

## Update your payment method
Go to Settings, then Billing, then Payment method, and add a new card. The new card is used from the
next charge.
