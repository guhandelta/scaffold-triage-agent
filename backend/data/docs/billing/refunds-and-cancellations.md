# Refunds and cancellations

## Refund requests
> Handled by a person.

Refunds, double charges and billing disputes are reviewed by our billing team. The agent never
promises or issues a refund; it hands the ticket to a person with the details.

## Cancel your subscription
Workspace admins can cancel from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. The workspace stays on the
paid plan until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the free plan. Data is kept.
