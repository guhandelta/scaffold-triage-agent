# Performance and outages

## Pages load slowly
Refresh the page, then try another browser or a private window. Large projects load faster when you
filter the view. If it stays slow, send us your browser and the project name.

## The service is down or many pages fail
> Handled by a person.

Site-wide outages are handled by our on-call engineers. The agent hands outage reports to a person
right away and does not guess at causes or timelines.
