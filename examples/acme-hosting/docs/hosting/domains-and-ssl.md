# Domains and SSL

## Point your domain to Acme Hosting
In your domain registrar, set the nameservers to ns1.acme-hosting.example and ns2.acme-hosting.example. Changes can take up to 24 hours to spread.

## Turn on free SSL
Open your site in the dashboard, choose Security, then turn on Free SSL. The certificate is issued within 15 minutes once the domain points to us.
